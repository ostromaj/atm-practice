# ATM Practice with Shelly 🐢

A touch-friendly ATM simulator for LFI students. Shelly the turtle walks students through every step, gives gentle hints when they get stuck, and hands out shells for finished lessons.

**Play:** https://ostromaj.github.io/atm-practice/

## What's inside

**Step 1: Learn with Shelly** (9 guided lessons)
1. Meet the ATM: tap the screen, card slot, keypad, cash slot and receipt slot
2. Put Your Card In: card in, CANCEL, take your card back
3. Your Secret PIN: cover the keypad, CLEAR, ENTER
4. Check Your Balance
5. Get Cash: card, cash, receipt
6. Other Amounts: typing $80
7. Put Money In: deposits and counting bills
8. Stay Safe: good choice or not?
9. Read a Receipt

**Step 2: Missions** (8 real-life tasks with a checklist): Lunch Money, Birthday Money, Shirt Shopping, Movie Night, Not Enough Money, Payday!, Grocery Run, Big Mission.

**Free Play ATM**: no rules, practice anything (PIN 1234).

## Teacher notes

- Turn on **Read to me** for students who need text read aloud. Every screen also has a 🔊 button.
- Wrong taps never fail a student. Shelly gives a hint and a glowing target after a second try.
- Progress is saved in the browser by first name. **My Shells → Show my teacher** prints a progress sheet.
- No flashing effects. Animations stop if the device has reduced motion turned on.
